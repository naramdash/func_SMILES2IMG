# Word Overview

Stop taking screenshots or fighting broken table layouts every time a substituent changes. Sketch, convert, style, and re-edit 2D structures, reaction schemes, and 3D protein figures directly inside Microsoft Word—with zero external drawing license and 100% standard `.docx` compatibility.

## See What Changes in Your Daily Manuscript Workflow

### 1. Double-Click In-Place Edits Without Breaking SAR Table Widths
Fix a methyl group or swap a catalyst inside a multi-row table just by **double-clicking** the structure. `func_SMILES2IMG` locks the aspect ratio and updates the image right inside the existing cell bounds.

<FeatureMedia id="word-ch3-compound-table" />

### 2. 1-Click ACS Monochrome Finish & Illustrator Vector `SVG` Before Submission
Finished drafting in color and need strict black-and-white line art for ACS, RSC, Wiley, or patent submission tonight? Convert every structure across your entire `.docx` (or a single highlighted table) in **one click**, or copy lossless vector `SVG` straight into Adobe Illustrator for TOC graphics.

<FeatureMedia id="word-ch6-publication-finish" />

### 3. Zero-Typo HRMS / Formula Copy & 3D Protein Figures (`Mol*`)
Pull Chemical Formula, Molecular Weight, Monoisotopic Exact Mass (`Da`), LogP, and Lipinski status straight from any structure into your Experimental section, or enter a 4-character PDB ID (`1CRN`, `6LU7`) to insert a crisp 3D protein figure.

## Your First 60 Seconds After Installing

1. **Try a 0.1-second conversion:** Type `Aspirin` or `CC(=O)Oc1ccccc1C(=O)O` in a blank Word paragraph, highlight it, and click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Paste Structure]`** (or right-click ➔ **`Chemical Structure` ➔ `Paste Structure`**).
2. **Sketch or edit in place:** **Double-click** that molecule to reopen **Ketcher 2D**, add a substituent, and press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd>—or click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Insert / Edit Structure]`** on an empty line to draw from scratch.
3. **Practice inside the self-contained Playbook (`.docx`):** Download <a href="/func_SMILES2IMG/examples/func_SMILES2IMG_Word_Playbook.docx" download="func_SMILES2IMG_Word_Playbook.docx"><strong>func_SMILES2IMG_Word_Playbook.docx</strong></a> and work through 6 guided pages of live SAR tables, KET reaction schemes, Mol\* `1CRN` figures, and 1-click ACS B&W conversion ([see 6-page breakdown →](/word/playbook)).
