# Insert & Paste Structures

Draw 2D molecules inline inside Word paragraphs with the offline **Ketcher 2D Editor**, or convert typed/clipboard SMILES text into crisp structures in 0.1 seconds.

## 1. Sketch an Inline Structure (`Ctrl + Shift + K`)

<FeatureMedia id="word-ch1-insert-ketcher" />

1. Place your cursor in the paragraph where the figure belongs.
2. Click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Insert / Edit Structure]`** (or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>).
3. Draw the molecule in Ketcher and press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd> to embed it inline with your text.

## 2. Convert Selected or Clipboard SMILES in 0.1s (`Paste Structure`)

<FeatureMedia id="word-ch2-quick-insert" />

1. Highlight any SMILES string in your Word document (e.g. `Cn1cnc2c1c(=O)n(C)c(=O)n2C`).
2. Click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Paste Structure]`** on the ribbon (or right-click ➔ **`Chemical Structure` ➔ `Paste Structure`**).
3. The selected text turns immediately into a crisp, aspect-ratio-locked inline structure. *(If no text is selected, `func_SMILES2IMG` converts the SMILES/InChI/KET currently on your clipboard.)*
