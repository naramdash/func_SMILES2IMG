# ACS B&W & Vector SVG

Before submitting a manuscript to ACS, Wiley, Elsevier, or RSC, normalize every chemical structure across all pages and tables in one click—or copy lossless vector SVG paths into Adobe Illustrator.

<FeatureMedia id="word-ch6-publication-finish" />

## 1. Whole-Manuscript Style Normalization (`Batch Style`)

1. Highlight specific paragraphs/tables to restyle **only your selection**, or leave nothing selected to convert **every chemical structure in the entire document**.
2. Click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Batch Style]`** and choose a preset:
   - **`Publication B&W`** — pure black high-contrast monochrome line art for print journals and patents
   - **`Standard Color`** — transparent background with IUPAC CPK element colors
   - **`White Background`** — solid opaque white rectangle (ideal inside shaded table rows)
   - **`Dark Mode`** — crisp white ink on transparent background

## 2. Copy Lossless Vector SVG (`Copy SVG`)

Select any structure in Word and click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Copy SVG]`** (or right-click ➔ **`Chemical Structure` ➔ `Copy Vector SVG`**), then press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>V</kbd> inside Adobe Illustrator, Inkscape, or vector editors. See [Style & Export](/editor/publishing) for full SVG and property copy details.
