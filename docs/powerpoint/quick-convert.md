# TextBox Quick Convert

Turn a PowerPoint TextBox containing a SMILES string or chemical name into a centered, aspect-ratio-locked 2D structure in 0.1 seconds.

<FeatureMedia id="powerpoint-slide-textbox-quick-insert" />

## Steps

1. Select a PowerPoint TextBox containing a SMILES string (e.g. `CC(=O)Oc1ccccc1C(=O)O`) or compound name (`Aspirin`).
2. Click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Paste Structure]`** on the ribbon (or right-click ➔ **`Chemical Structure` ➔ `Paste Structure`**).
3. PowerPoint places the rendered molecule directly on your slide with locked aspect ratio (`msoTrue`). *(If no TextBox is selected, `func_SMILES2IMG` converts chemical text currently on your clipboard.)*
