# Live Q&A Molecule Info

Inspect all 17 physicochemical, Lipinski, and 3D complexity descriptors directly from any structure shape on your slide—or paste them as bulleted slide text in one click.

<FeatureMedia id="powerpoint-slide-live-qa-info" />

## Steps

1. Select any chemical structure on your slide and click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Molecule Info]`** (or right-click ➔ **`Chemical Structure` ➔ `Copy Molecule Info`**).
2. Choose **`Copy as Multi-Line List`** to paste clean bulleted descriptors (Formula, MW, Exact Mass, LogP, TPSA, Lipinski) straight into a PowerPoint TextBox.
3. Open **`Detailed Molecule Info Window...`** during live Q&A or slide prep to inspect the 2D preview and double-click any individual row to copy a single value.
