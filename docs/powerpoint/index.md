### 1. Zero White Boxes + 1-Click Whole-Deck Theme Switching (`Batch Style`)
Need to move dark conference slides into a bright lab-meeting template or print monochrome handouts tonight? Switch **every 2D structure across your entire presentation** between Dark Mode, Standard Color, ACS B&W, and White Card in a single click—keeping every coordinate and alignment intact.

## Your First 60 Seconds After Installing

1. **Convert a TextBox in 0.1s:** Insert a TextBox on any slide, type `Aspirin` or `CC(=O)Oc1ccccc1C(=O)O`, select the TextBox, and click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Paste Structure]`** (or right-click ➔ **`Chemical Structure` ➔ `Paste Structure`**).
2. **Switch to Dark Mode & re-edit in place:** On a dark slide, click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Batch Style]` ➔ `[Dark Mode]`**, then **double-click** the structure to open **Ketcher 2D** and tweak a group in place.
3. **Practice inside the 8-slide Playbook (`.pptx`):** Download <a href="/func_SMILES2IMG/examples/func_SMILES2IMG_PowerPoint_Playbook.pptx" download="func_SMILES2IMG_PowerPoint_Playbook.pptx"><strong>func_SMILES2IMG_PowerPoint_Playbook.pptx</strong></a> and open it with Speaker Notes visible to practice 0.1s TextBox conversion, 4-card grids, KET reactions, Mol\* `1CRN`, and deck-wide restyling ([see 8-slide breakdown →](/powerpoint/playbook)).
