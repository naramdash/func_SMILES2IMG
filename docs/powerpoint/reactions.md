# Reaction Mechanism Slides

Build multi-step organic synthesis and mechanism slides with reaction arrows, `+` signs, and catalyst annotations—persisted losslessly inside PowerPoint shape metadata (`Shape.Tags["KET"]`).

<FeatureMedia id="powerpoint-slide-reaction" />

## Steps

1. Click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Insert / Edit Structure]`** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>).
2. Draw reactants, reaction arrows (`➔`), `+` signs, and catalyst/condition labels (`T`) in **Ketcher 2D**.
3. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd> to place the scheme onto your slide.
4. **Double-click** the reaction graphic anytime later to adjust arrows or reagents in place without moving your slide layout.
