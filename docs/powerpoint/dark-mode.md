# Dark Mode & Screening Cards

Place alpha-transparent white-ink chemical structures onto dark PowerPoint templates with zero white bounding boxes.

## 1. 4-Card Lead Compound Screening Grid

<FeatureMedia id="powerpoint-slide-candidate-grid" />

1. Set your slide background to dark navy or slate (e.g. `#0F172A`) and lay out compound cards.
2. Insert each compound via **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Paste Structure]`** or **`[Insert / Edit Structure]`**.
3. Click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Batch Style]` ➔ `[Dark Mode]`** to turn all slide structures into crisp white-ink transparent graphics.

## 2. Transparent White-Ink Canvas

<FeatureMedia id="powerpoint-slide-dark-mode" />

* **Inside Ketcher:** Toggle **`[Background: Transparent]`** in Ketcher's top toolbar before clicking **`Apply to Slide`**.
* **Batch Convert Anytime:** Select specific slide shapes—or leave none selected to convert the **entire deck**—and click **`[Batch Style]` ➔ `[Dark Mode]`**.
