# 3D Protein Slides (`Mol*`)

Embed interactive **360°-rotatable 3D protein models (`.glb`)** directly onto PowerPoint slides—or export transparent high-DPI 2D protein renders—using the built-in **Mol\*** viewer.

<FeatureMedia id="powerpoint-slide-molstar-3d" />

## Steps

1. Click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[3D Structure (Mol*)]`** on the PowerPoint ribbon.
2. Enter a 4-letter PDB code (`1CRN`, `4HHB`, `6LU7`, `1BNA`) and click **`Fetch`**, or click **`Open File...`** to load a local `.pdb` / `.cif` / `.sdf` file.
3. Choose the output mode and click **`Apply to Slide`** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd>):
   - **`3D Model (GLB)`** — drag to rotate live in 360° right on your slide (PowerPoint 2019+/365; works even on Mac/conference PCs without the add-in)
   - **`2D Picture`** — high-resolution PNG render (`BG: Transparent` recommended for dark slides)
4. **Double-click** any inserted 3D model or 2D render later to reopen Mol\* at the exact same camera angle and visual style.
