# Deck-Wide Batch Style & SVG

Switch every chemical structure across an entire presentation between Dark Mode, Standard Color, Publication B&W, and White Background in one click—or copy lossless vector SVG for A0 conference posters.

<FeatureMedia id="powerpoint-slide-batch-style" />

## 1. Restyle Selected Shapes or the Entire Deck (`Batch Style`)

1. Select specific shapes on one slide to convert **only your selection**, or leave nothing selected to transform **every chemical structure across all slides**.
2. Click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[Batch Style]`** and pick **`Standard Color`**, **`Publication B&W`**, **`White Background`**, or **`Dark Mode`**.

## 2. Right-Click Context Menu & Poster SVG

![Live 3-Level Chemical Structure Context Menu in PowerPoint](/powerpoint-context-menu-guide.png)

* Right-click any slide structure ➔ **`Chemical Structure` ➔ `Copy Vector SVG`** to paste editable vector paths directly into Adobe Illustrator, Inkscape, or large-format A0 conference posters.
* Click **`[SMILES]` ➔ `[About func_SMILES2IMG]`** anytime to verify version info or check for updates.
